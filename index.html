Remediation centre

Possible fixes, recommended actions and agent-assisted plans.

Human approval requiredAgent actions are recommendations only. No commands are executed and no production changes are made.
CRITICAL · DEMO

Suspicious privileged sign-in

Identity service · Multiple failed logins followed by a successful sign-in from an unfamiliar location.

Possible fixes

  • Revoke active sessions and rotate affected credentials.
  • Enforce phishing-resistant MFA for privileged accounts.
  • Review sign-in logs and conditional-access policies.

Recommended response

  1. Validate the alert with the identity/security team.
  2. Contain the account under the approved incident playbook.
  3. Preserve logs and investigate lateral movement.
HIGH · DEMO

Endpoint malware detection

PNG-HQ-WS-042 · Behaviour resembles ransomware staging.

Possible fixes

  • Isolate endpoint using the authorised EDR console.
  • Quarantine confirmed malicious files.
  • Verify backup integrity and scan connected storage.

Agent-assisted plan

  1. Collect endpoint telemetry and process tree.
  2. Correlate hashes against trusted threat intelligence.
  3. Prepare isolation and recovery steps for analyst approval.
